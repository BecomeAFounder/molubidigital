Common questions

Do I need to be on TikTok already?

No. If you are not on TikTok yet, we set up your profile and plan your first videos in the opening sessions.

What does a website with no backend mean?

It means the site is simple and fast, with no database or server for you to look after. Your contact form sends enquiries straight to you.

How soon will I see results?

That depends on your industry and how regularly you post. In the first week we record where you are today, and each month we report against that, so you can see what has changed.

What happens after the three months?

Your team carries on posting on its own. Most clients keep us on for website management so the site keeps improving.

Can I buy just the website or just the training?

Yes. Each service stands on its own.

Do you guarantee sales?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. What we commit to is a clear goal, honest monthly reporting and careful work.
